The Foibles Atlas

Fifty-two things students say, arranged not by the topic they get wrong but by the kind of thing they have taken a quantity to be. Each entry asks what the speaker must believe for the sentence to make sense, before it offers any correction at all.

Lee McCulloch-James · Ontological fluency in school physics

The diagnostic question is not which fact has been forgotten. It is what must this student believe this quantity is, for their answer to make sense?

Every card below withholds its diagnosis until you ask for it. Read the utterance, decide for yourself which category has slipped, then reveal. The order matters: a correction issued before the diagnosis usually changes the answer while leaving the ontology intact, which is why the same error returns three weeks later wearing a different topic.
