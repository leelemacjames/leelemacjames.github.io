Teacher reveal and mathematical extension

For any network with V dots, E links and C separate connected groups, the number of independent loops is:

independent loops = E − V + C

When the network is connected, (C=1). Twelve dots and eleven links therefore give no independent loops; twelve links give one.

The social model adds exactly one link with every new user, so it remains a tree even when one user becomes a large hub. Preferential attachment mainly favours users who are already established. The platform-boost rule is included to show how a late arrival can become a hub through extra visibility.
