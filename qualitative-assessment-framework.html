The LENS × ARC framework

Beyond the Single Grade

Intellectual quality through a LENS; intellectual agency along an ARC

The problem is resolution

A numbered grade looks like a measurement. A 6 appears to be exactly one unit better than a 5, even where two careful markers could place the same response on either side of the boundary.

Qualitative assessment often resolves around a region rather than a point. The 1–9 scale is a micrometer engraved on a rubber ruler. It turns a coarse judgement into a sharp-looking integer, then treats the boundary as fact, giving a broad human judgement ("appraisal") the faux costume of a laboratory reading.

The linked DeepMark article serves as our provocation. Ofqual's position is subtler: an adjacent mark may be equally defensible, particularly in extended responses. That nuance strengthens the case for reporting the kind of thinking demonstrated, at a vaguer resolution the evidence can bear under scrutiny.

Human marking scatters. AI marking clumps. The tight clump has real value, although it may still sit off the bullseye. A machine can miss with remarkable discipline.

The proposal

Coarse-grain the judgement and report two separate features of the response. The first records what the thinking does. The second records its relationship to the supplied, taught or shared line.

The framework describes this piece of work while not pretending to turn a passing performance into a personality type.

The two axes

Vertical axis

LENS: intellectual quality

The strongest move visible: Lay out, Examine, Notice or Sift.

Horizontal axis

ARC: intellectual agency

How the response handles an available line: Adopt, Rework or Challenge.

The rows indicate the strongest move demonstrated. They are broad descriptors rather than evenly spaced numerical units. A response may contain evidence from several rows. The two-letter code records LENS first and ARC second: ER means Examine × Rework; SC means Sift × Challenge.

Insufficient evidence sits outside the grid

A blank, fragmentary or withheld response supports no claim about intellectual quality. Record what was not demonstrated. Avoid converting absence of evidence into a diagnosis of inability, indifference or disengagement. Engagement can be recorded separately as an observed pattern across time.

Reporting the judgement

Combine one LENS descriptor with one ARC descriptor, cite the move that supports it, then name one realistic next move.

LENS + ARC + evidence + next move

ER · Examine × Rework. Develops the shared account by making one significant causal relationship explicit.

Next move towards SR: sift that explanation against a plausible alternative and state why one deserves greater confidence.
