How physicists and biologists describe the same phenomena, and why only one of them can write an equation

The Question

Why can physicists write equations, and biologists mostly cannot?

The standard answer invokes complexity: biological systems have too many variables. That is true and insufficient. The deeper reason is closure.

Where a system's degrees of freedom can be isolated and its symmetries identified, description collapses into algebra. Where they cannot, explanation must be unfolded into process.

Three Compressions
F = q v × B
Three vectors, one relation, complete. The cross product encodes a three-way directional constraint.
a = GM / r²
The mass cancels. Universality falls out of the cancellation and is later elevated to geometry.
ε = − dΦ/dt
A single sign enforces conservation of energy, and with it opposition and causation.

Nothing is lost in these compressions. The mechanism is not omitted from the equation; it is implicit in the structure of the equation.

The Thesis
Physics retains what must be true. Biology reconstructs how it came to be true.

Physics is amenable to mathematisation not because physicists are cleverer, but because the systems they study are closed enough to admit algebraic structure. When variables can be isolated, interactions are few-to-few, and conservation laws constrain the space of possibilities, description compresses to equation.

When variables proliferate, interactions are many-to-many, and context dominates, description must expand into narrative.

Example 01 · Lenz's law and eddy currents
Compression
ε = − dΦB/dt

The minus sign does all the explanatory work. It encodes opposition, causation and energy conservation simultaneously. Without it, induced currents would reinforce the change in flux and produce energy from nothing.

Eddy currents are the distributed realisation of this relation in a conductor. Direction, causation, constraint and dissipation are all implicit.

What is invariant here?
Expansion

A changing magnetic environment induces circulating currents that act to stabilise the system against further change, dissipating energy as heat.

The minus sign becomes negative feedback, directly analogous to homeostasis or feedback inhibition in a metabolic pathway. The flux derivative becomes a perturbation triggering a distributed response.

What is the conductivity? What geometry lets loops form? What breaks first?
Invariance → stability under perturbation  ·  algebraic necessity → thermodynamic cost

Example 02 · Mass defect and binding energy
Compression
Δm = Zmp + Nmn − mnuc
Eb = Δm c²

The missing mass is not missing; it is energy already released during formation. The strong interaction lowers the system's energy and the bound system sits in a potential well.

The equation is the mechanism, once written at the right level of abstraction.

What quantity is conserved?
Expansion

Separate nucleons interact through short-range attractive forces, rearrange into a more stable configuration, and release energy to the surroundings, leaving a lower-energy bound structure.

The scalar Eb becomes a transition between states, and the equality becomes a question about pathways: gamma emission, kinetic recoil, intermediate configurations. The ground state becomes a position in a stability landscape, like a protein folding funnel.

What is the proton-to-neutron ratio? Which shells are closed?
A number that looks static encodes a historical process: energy released during formation

Example 03 · Specific heat capacity
Compression
CV = (∂U/∂T)V

Heat capacity measures the number of accessible degrees of freedom weighted by their coupling strengths. The hydrogen bonding network supplies an extensive reservoir of intermolecular modes.

Energy is absorbed into translation, rotation, and the excitation, bending and partial disruption of an extended fluctuating network. The high value follows from the count of coupled modes per unit mass.

How many degrees of freedom?
Expansion

Thermal inertia is a functional property with ecological consequences. Oceans buffer planetary temperature. Blood, lymph and cytoplasm act as thermal reservoirs resisting rapid fluctuation.

The hydrogen bond network becomes an adaptive infrastructure: a cooperative, dynamic lattice that absorbs perturbation and underwrites homeostasis from the cell to the planet.

What does this stability enable?
Density of accessible modes → what the property affords

Light through two slits produces bright and dark fringes.
Example 04 · Double-slit interference
Compression
d sinθ = nλ

Positions, spacings and relative intensities of every maximum and minimum are determined by superposition and the geometry of path difference. With finite slit width the envelope combines diffraction and interference in a single closed expression.

Geometry, superposition, and one wavelength.

What relation must hold?
Expansion

A wave meets a barrier with two openings, passes through both, and the contributions recombine. Where they arrive in phase the signal is strong; where out of phase it cancels. The fringe pattern is the record of that recombination.

The pattern is fragile and conditional. Change the coherence length, the slit width or the wavelength and it degrades. In the single-particle case it builds statistically and vanishes when which-path information is available.

Under what conditions does the pattern survive?
A closed predictive encoding → a regime-specific outcome of source, barrier and detector

Example 05 · Free-fall equivalence
Compression
ma = GMm/r²  ⇒  a = GM/r²

The mass cancels. That cancellation reflects the equivalence of inertial and gravitational mass, which Einstein elevated to a principle and made the foundation of general relativity.

Geometrically, free fall is not acceleration under a force but geodesic motion: all objects follow the same paths because gravity is the curvature of the arena rather than a force acting on mass.

What cancels, and what does the cancellation mean?
Expansion

What medium? What drag? What Reynolds number? The universality is real, and it is the universality of an idealised limit. In any actual context, air resistance, buoyancy, terminal velocity and morphology dominate.

Falling is an ecological event: a seed disperses, a predator dives, a fruit detaches. A samara autorotates, a peregrine tucks its wings, a spider pays out silk to catch the wind. Each is falling, and equivalence is the least interesting part.

What happens when the stripped-away complexity is restored?
The invariant is the starting point for one discipline and the idealised limit for the other

Bulbs in parallel burn brighter and flatten the battery faster.
Example 06 · Series and parallel circuits
Compression
Pseries = ε²/NR
Pparallel = Nε²/R

In series the total resistance is NR, the current falls, and each bulb receives ε²/N²R. In parallel each bulb sees the full voltage and dissipates ε²/R, so total power rises linearly with N.

Why parallel bulbs are brighter, why the battery drains faster, and by exactly how much, all sit inside Ohm's law and P = IV.

What does the topology do to the algebra?
Expansion

A resource distribution problem. The battery is a finite store, the bulbs are consumers, and the wiring topology determines how the resource is partitioned.

Series is a single supply chain, like flux through a linear metabolic pathway. Parallel is independent lines from a shared reservoir, like capillary beds branching from an artery. Parallel access maximises per-unit output and accelerates depletion.

Who draws from what, and how fast does the reservoir empty?
An equation → a supply-and-demand architecture: distribution sets the depletion rate

Description and Explanation

The distinction is not description versus mechanism. It is that in physics, at the fundamental level, the distinction collapses.

Under closure, a good description is already a mechanistic explanation, because the equation carries the mechanism inside its structure. In biology the two remain distinct layers, and a description that is not also an explanation is the normal case rather than a failure.

Renaming a phenomenon is not explaining it. But writing the right relation sometimes is, and knowing which situation you are in is the skill.

Why Closure Fails in Biology

Life on Earth is the product of Darwinian selection acting on accident. The organisms, pathways and structures a biologist studies were not derived from first principles; they were arrived at through a contingent, branching history of variation and differential survival.

Their forms are not necessary but selected. The explanations therefore mirror the origin: expanded, conditional, narrative, context-dependent.

The narrative character of biology is a faithful reflection of the contingency of its subject matter.

Two Profiles

Physics

  • Minimal variables.
  • Exact relations, invariant under transformation.
  • Mechanism compressed into algebra.
  • Description is explanation.

Biology

  • Many variables.
  • Interaction networks, context-dependent.
  • Mechanism expanded into narrative.
  • Description and explanation remain distinct layers.

Neither mode is superior. They are adapted to different regimes of the same explanatory landscape.

The Axis Is Closure, Not Difficulty

Ask of any system: can the variables be isolated? Are interactions few-to-few? Do conservation laws constrain what is possible? Is the behaviour independent of the system's history?

Where the answers are yes, compression is available and the equation will carry the mechanism. Where they are no, expansion is not a stylistic retreat but the only faithful account, and demanding an equation would discard exactly the information that matters.

Physics compresses to invariants. Biology expands to dependencies.

Test the Claim

The interesting cases sit on the boundary. Ask where each of these falls, and what closure it does or does not enjoy:

  • Statistical mechanics of a gas.
  • Population genetics under selection.
  • Fluid turbulence.
  • Epidemiological spread.
  • Chemical kinetics.
  • Plate tectonics.
  • Allometric scaling laws.
  • Climate models.

Several of these compress partially: an invariant governs the aggregate while the individual trajectory stays irreducibly narrative. Naming which layer is compressible is a better answer than assigning the whole field to one column.

The Diagnostic Exercise

For each phenomenon, four descriptions. Two are genuine, two are plausible impostors.

One genuine account is the physicist's compressed invariant, the other the biologist's expanded mechanism. Both are physically correct and differ only in explanatory mode. The impostors read fluently and contain substantive errors. Tag each one, then check your reading.

Conclusion
A cross product encodes a three-way interaction. A mass cancellation encodes geometric universality. A minus sign encodes conservation.

Where the system is closed, that is the whole explanation. Where closure fails, the same phenomena re-described necessarily expand into process, narrative and conditionality, and the expansion is not a lesser account of a simple thing but an honest account of a complicated one.
