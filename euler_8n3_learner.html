Euler's 8n + 3 Conjecture

Every number of the form 8n + 3 seems to be a square plus twice a prime. You will find the shape of the representations, watch several natural guesses fall, and build a counting law that predicts how many representations each number has.

Seed: Euler's conjecture as quoted, with his table for n = 1, …, 10 and the remark that he verified it for every number of the form 8n + 3 below 200, from a supplied excerpt of an introductory number-theory text.
