Euler's 8n + 3 Conjecture on the Spirals

Lay the integers along an Ulam spiral on the Gaussian lattice, whose units are the four fourth roots of unity, or along the Eisenstein-Ulam spiral on the hexagonal lattice, whose units are the six sixth roots of unity (the three cube roots of unity and their negatives). Then watch where the primes p of Euler's representations N = a² + 2p fall.
