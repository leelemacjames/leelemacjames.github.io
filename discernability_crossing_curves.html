The Claim As Reported

In April 2023 the United Nations Population Division announced that India's population would reach:

1,425,775,850
Ten significant figures

Nobody counted anybody that month. The last Indian census was taken in 2011; the last Chinese census in November 2020. The figure is the output of a model, quoted to the nearest fifty people.

A number's precision is a claim about the method, not a courtesy of the arithmetic.

What Was Actually Observed

Two censuses, taken years apart, in different decades, under different administrations.

Everything reported for 2023 is what happens when you take a base count, attach rates of change measured on samples, and integrate forward. The further you are from the census, the more of the answer is model and the less of it is observation.

The crossing date was never measured. It was extrapolated.

The Governing Relation
δt  =  δ(NI − NC)  ÷  |d(NI − NC)/dt| Uncertainty in the crossing date equals uncertainty in the gap divided by the rate at which the gap closes

A crossing date is not knowable to better precision than the uncertainty in the difference, divided by the speed of approach. If two populations of 1.4 billion are each known to within half a percent, the difference is uncertain by roughly ten million people, and ten million people is around nine months of closing.

When two curves cross at a shallow angle, the date is the least well determined thing about them.

Push the uncertainty to two or three percent, which is not unreasonable for a population last enumerated more than a decade ago, and the crossing becomes a multi-year window rather than a month.

And Indeed
One published demographic analysis, working from India's own official series and arguing that the two countries' data quality must be adjusted differently before they are compared at all, places the crossover in 2026 rather than 2023.
Demography India, vol. 52 no. 1 (2023)

The disagreement is roughly three years. That is not a dispute about arithmetic. It is a dispute about how much to adjust each census for undercount, and whether one country's figures have been taken more nearly at face value than the other's.

A crossing date is an artefact of the correction model, and the correction model is a judgement.

What The Evidence Actually Warrants

Well supported

  • The two populations are of the same order and differ by a few million at most.
  • India's is rising; China's has begun to fall.
  • Therefore a crossing occurs, and occurs once.
  • It falls somewhere in the first half of the 2020s.
Robust to the correction model

Not supported

  • A specific month.
  • A figure quoted to the last fifty people.
  • The implication that something was counted.
  • Any statement of the form “on this day it happened”.
An artefact of quoting a model output verbatim

The direction was discernible long before the date was, and the date may never be.

A Harder Question, Deliberately Absurd

Are there more hairs on Indian heads than on Chinese heads?

Nobody has ever counted. There is no census of follicles. And yet the question has a determinate answer, and we can ask how close we could get to it.

Two Routes to an Answer

Direct estimation

  • Sample scalps; count follicles in a measured area; scale by scalp area.
  • Trichological studies do this, on samples of dozens or hundreds.
  • Sampling frame is clinical, therefore not representative of a population.
  • Measurement is expensive and the sample will always be tiny.
Small n, direct construct, poor representativeness

Factor decomposition

  • N̄hairs = population × mean hairs per head.
  • Mean hairs per head modelled from age structure, sex ratio, alopecia prevalence, follicular density, nutrition.
  • Each factor measured on a larger and better sample.
  • But the model linking them is itself an assumption.
Large n per factor, indirect construct, model risk
The Error Budget
Population figures~ 0.5 – 3 %
Sex and age structure~ 2 – 5 %
Prevalence of androgenetic alopecia by age band~ 10 – 20 %
Mean follicular density and scalp area~ 20 – 30 %
Representativeness of the trichological sampleunquantified

The demographic term, which felt like the uncertain one in the population question, is here the best known quantity in the calculation by a factor of thirty.

The Counterintuitive Result

The population gap between the two countries is around two tenths of one percent, and our uncertainty is comparable to it, so the sign is barely resolvable.

The hair question is far worse measured in absolute terms, but the two countries differ markedly in median age, and older populations carry substantially more androgenetic alopecia. If that effect moves mean hairs per head by several percent, it dwarfs the population gap entirely, and the sign of the comparison is decided by a factor we can bound even though we cannot measure it well.

A badly measured quantity with a large true difference can be more discernible than a well measured quantity with a tiny one.

Discernability Is a Ratio
D  =  |A − B|  ÷  √(σA2 + σB2) Signal separation over combined uncertainty

Whether a question is answerable does not depend on how precisely each side is known. It depends on how the separation compares with the uncertainty. Precision is only worth buying where the separation is small.

Ask not how well we know each quantity, but how well we know the difference.

Direct Versus Factor-Based Methods

Direct measurement inherits

  • Sampling error, which shrinks predictably with n.
  • Selection bias, which does not shrink at all.
  • A construct that matches the question exactly.
Fails through unrepresentativeness

Factor decomposition inherits

  • Errors in every factor, which compound multiplicatively.
  • The structural assumption that the factors are the right ones and combine that way.
  • Access to much larger samples per factor.
Fails through model misspecification

Neither is safer in principle. Agreement between them is worth more than either alone, because their failure modes are unrelated.

Interpolation, Extrapolation, Induction
—
Between two censusesInterpolation. Constrained at both ends. Errors in the assumed path are bounded by the endpoints.
—
After the last censusExtrapolation. Constrained at one end only. Errors accumulate without correction, and there is nothing downstream to catch them.
—
Assuming rates persistInduction. The claim that the mechanism which generated the past sample continues to generate the future. Not itself derivable from the sample.

The India figure for 2023 was twelve years of one-sided extrapolation resting on an inductive assumption about fertility decline.

Why Precision Gets Overstated

A model returns a number with as many digits as the arithmetic supplies. Rounding it to the honest resolution requires a deliberate act, and that act is where the uncertainty gets lost.

The reporting chain compounds this. The originating body states that the date is approximate and subject to revision; the summary states the date; the headline states the day. Each step is defensible on its own and the sequence destroys the caveat.

Uncertainty is not usually removed by anyone. It is simply not carried forward.

The Discernability Check
What was counted, when, and by whom?
How much of this figure is observation and how much is model?
Over what interval has the model been integrated without correction?
Is the estimate inside or outside the observed range?
What is the separation, and what is the combined uncertainty?
Does the claimed precision exceed the resolution of the method?
Would an independent method, with unrelated failure modes, agree?
Which single assumption, if wrong, would move the answer most?
What would the honest rounding of this number look like?
Two Questions, One Structure

The population question looks respectable and is nearly unanswerable at the resolution it is reported. The hair question looks frivolous and may be answerable in sign, because the quantity being compared is genuinely different between the two groups.

Seriousness of subject is not the same as strength of evidence, and the appearance of rigour is not the same as resolution.

What To Carry Away

Every number about a population is an integrated estimate, and every crossing date is that estimate divided by a rate.

Ask what was observed, over what interval it has been carried forward, how large the difference is compared with the error on the difference, and whether the digits being quoted could survive a second, unrelated method being pointed at the same question.
