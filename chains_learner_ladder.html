Chains of Consecutive Numbers

Some numbers are sums of consecutive positive integers. You will find out which ones, how long the sums are and in how many ways, by noticing patterns, framing conjectures and testing them, some of which will fall.

Seed problem: John Mason, with Leone Burton and Kaye Stacey, Thinking Mathematically, Addison-Wesley (1982), the Consecutive Sums investigation, with the examples 9 = 2+3+4, 11 = 5+6 and 18 = 3+4+5+6.
