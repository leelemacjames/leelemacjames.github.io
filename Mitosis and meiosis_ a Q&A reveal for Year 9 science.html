Questions marked “Core” cover the main ideas for Year 9, and questions marked “Going further” stretch beyond them. The “Read first” passages are simplified summaries written for this page. The diagrams are simplified schematics of an imaginary cell with 4 chromosomes, drawn so that the stages are easy to compare, and real cells have more chromosomes and look messier under the microscope. The history of the discoveries is summarised from standard sources such as biography pages from the DNA Learning Center, the Embryo Project Encyclopedia and university biology notes, and dates are given as they are usually reported.
